GetUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. About GetUp

GetUp is a task-based alarm application designed to help users build a consistent waking routine. It may let users configure alarms, complete wake-up tasks, scan registered QR codes or barcodes, and review routine history.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, GetUp may store alarm schedules and settings, registered QR codes or barcodes, task preferences, completion status, routine history, and locally cached Pro entitlement information. This information supports the app's functionality and is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

3. Camera and scanning

If you choose to scan a QR code or barcode, GetUp uses your device's camera for that task. The scanned value is used to check the code registered for the task. The camera is not intended for advertising or facial recognition.

4. Alarms and device permissions

GetUp uses relevant operating-system capabilities to schedule alarms, play alarm sounds, display alarm screens, and perform follow-up checks. These functions support the app's core purpose. Alarm and task information is processed locally by the app and relevant operating-system components.

5. Purchases

GetUp may offer a one-time Pro purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. GetUp does not receive your full payment-card details, but the app may receive purchase-related information, such as ownership status, to determine whether Pro features are available. Google's handling of purchase information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Data collection, sharing, and advertising

GetUp is designed to operate without a GetUp-operated account system or application backend. The intended release configuration does not send alarm schedules, registered codes, or routine history to GetUp servers. GetUp does not sell personal information or use alarm history for advertising.

Google Play may process information associated with app distribution, diagnostics, and purchases under Google's own policies. The app's actual behavior and third-party SDKs must be considered when assessing these statements.

7. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until deleted within the app, where supported, or until the app and its local data are removed. Device backups and operating-system features may affect retention. Google Play may retain purchase records under its own policies and legal obligations.

8. Security

GetUp relies on private app storage and device operating-system security features to help protect locally stored information. No method of storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

GetUp is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Its intended audience and applicable age-related requirements are declared separately in Google Play Console.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will be available at this webpage.
