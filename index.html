GetUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GetUp handles information when you use the GetUp task-based alarm application ("GetUp" or "the App").

1. About GetUp

GetUp helps you wake up by completing tasks, such as scanning a registered QR code or barcode. Depending on the features you use, the App can manage alarm schedules, wake-up tasks, follow-up checks, and routine history.

GetUp is designed to operate primarily on your device without a GetUp-operated user account or application backend.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

GetUp stores information locally to provide its features. Depending on your use of the App, this may include:

  • Alarm schedules and alarm settings.
  • Registered QR codes or barcodes used for wake-up tasks.
  • Task preferences and selected difficulty settings.
  • Alarm completion status and follow-up check results.
  • Routine and alarm history.
  • Locally stored Pro entitlement and related app preferences.

This information is used to operate the App and is stored in the App's local storage. GetUp does not intentionally upload alarm schedules, registered wake-up codes, or routine history to GetUp-operated servers.

3. Camera and Barcode Scanning

GetUp uses your device's camera when you choose to scan a registered QR code or barcode to complete a wake-up task.

The App checks the scanned value against the code registered for your task. Registered code values are stored locally for this purpose.

Camera access is used for scanning, not for advertising or facial recognition. GetUp does not intentionally save camera frames or photos as part of the normal scanning workflow.

GetUp uses Google's ML Kit barcode-scanning technology. The SDK processes camera input to recognize codes. Google's published documentation describes technical data that ML Kit may collect for diagnostics and usage analytics. Details are provided in Section 6.

4. Alarms and Device Permissions

GetUp uses supported operating-system features and permissions to schedule alarms, play alarm sounds, present alarm screens, access the camera when scanning, and perform configured follow-up checks.

Alarm schedules, task settings, and routine history are processed locally by the App. Alarm delivery can also depend on your device's operating system, permission settings, battery restrictions, and manufacturer-specific behavior.

5. Purchases and GetUp Pro

GetUp may offer an optional one-time purchase to unlock GetUp Pro features. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

Google Play handles payment processing and related purchase records under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. GetUp does not receive your full payment-card details.

The App may receive purchase information needed to determine whether Pro features are available, such as the purchase status or entitlement. The App stores relevant entitlement information locally to make Pro features available.

For more information about Google's practices, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's Terms of Service.

6. Data Collection, Sharing, and Advertising

GetUp's own operations

GetUp is designed to operate without a GetUp-operated account system or application backend. GetUp does not intentionally send alarm schedules, registered wake-up codes, or routine history to GetUp servers.

GetUp does not sell personal information or use alarm history for advertising. The App does not include advertising as a feature of its intended release configuration.

Barcode scanning and technical diagnostics

GetUp uses Google's ML Kit barcode-scanning SDK. According to Google's published Android data disclosure documentation, ML Kit may collect technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics, including:

  • Device information, such as manufacturer, model, and operating-system version.
  • App information, such as package name and app version.
  • Per-installation identifiers used for diagnostics and analytics.
  • Performance metrics, such as processing latency.
  • Technical configuration information, including image format and resolution.
  • Feature events, feature versions, input or output sizes, and error codes.

GetUp's barcode scanner has auto-zoom enabled. Google's documentation identifies additional technical information associated with auto-zoom, including a dynamically generated scanning-session identifier, zoom-level changes, and predicted coordinates of a bounding box that may contain a barcode.

This technical information is distinct from the registered wake-up code and routine history stored by GetUp. This policy describes the practices published by Google; it does not independently verify which telemetry is transmitted by every version or configuration of the App.

Google states that the ML Kit data described in its documentation is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred to third parties. Google's handling of such information is subject to its applicable documentation and privacy terms.

Read Google's ML Kit Android data disclosure for further details.

Sharing initiated by you

If you choose to print or share a wake-up code, GetUp may create a temporary PDF containing the code and its name. The operating system or the application you select to receive or print the file may handle it according to that provider's own practices.

GetUp does not control how an external application, printing service, or recipient handles information after you choose to share it.

Google Play services

Google Play may process information associated with app distribution, diagnostics, and purchases under Google's own policies. Information handled by Google or other platform providers is subject to their applicable privacy terms.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by GetUp remains on your device until you delete it through available App features or remove the App's local data or uninstall the App, as applicable.

Deleting the App or its local data may remove locally stored alarms, registered codes, preferences, and history. Before deleting data or uninstalling the App, consider whether you need to retain any information.

Device backups, operating-system features, and external applications may affect how long copies of information remain outside GetUp's direct control.

Google Play may retain purchase and transaction records under its own retention practices and legal obligations. Deleting local App data does not necessarily delete those records.

8. Data Security

GetUp is designed to keep its alarm schedules, registered codes, settings, and routine history in local App storage. Device-level security features help protect locally stored information.

Google states that the ML Kit diagnostic data described in its documentation is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

No method of storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your device protected and install operating-system and App updates when available.

9. Children's Privacy

GetUp is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children through a GetUp-operated account or backend.

Parents and guardians should consider the App's functionality, applicable age requirements, and the terms of the platform through which it is distributed before allowing a child to use it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, third-party SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

You should review this page periodically to stay informed about the App's privacy practices.
